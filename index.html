E-Vedhika: Comprehensive Digital Portal for Panchayat Secretaries

E-Vedhika is an innovative digital platform designed to assist Panchayat Secretaries in Telangana. Our portal simplifies the management of village-level data, bringing various departmental formats, UBD trackers, and farmer registries into a single unified workspace.

By automating calculations and generating instant reports, E-Vedhika bridges the gap between complex governmental requirements and efficient local administration. Discover how we can help streamline your daily workflow.
